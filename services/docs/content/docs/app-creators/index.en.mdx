This section is for app owners, developers, subject-matter experts, and editors who publish or maintain a solution in the JustApps catalog. The goal is more than a complete record: another person should be able to understand, try, and operate the app safely.

## The complete path

| Phase          | What you do                                                   | Guide                                                                    |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1. Prepare     | Clarify audience, ownership, source code, and operating model | [Workflow and publication](/app-creators/workflow)                       |
| 2. Describe    | Enter name, value, status, categories, license, and links     | [Content and metadata](/app-creators/content-and-metadata)               |
| 3. Deploy      | Document Docker, Compose, or Helm with reproducible details   | [Deployment guides](/app-creators/deployment-guides)                     |

The usual entry point is **Meine Apps → Neue App**. Administrators can also use [Verwaltung → Katalog → App hinzufügen](/verwaltung/katalog/apps/new).

## Before creating the app

Have these details ready:

- an understandable name and stable technical ID;
- a short, honest description of the value;
- at least one suitable category;
- status, audience, owner, and contact;
- license and source-code link;
- a working demo or production link, if available;
- requirements such as databases, Secrets, external services, and permissions;
- reproducible start or installation steps.

If the app is not ready for use, choose **Entwurf**, **POC**, or **Sandbox** instead of presenting it as established. A clearly marked unfinished entry is more useful than a finished-looking guide that cannot be started.

## Good app entries

A good entry quickly answers:

1. What problem does the app solve?
2. Who may or should use it?
3. What data does it process?
4. How can I test it?
5. How do I install and update it?
6. Who can answer questions and who is responsible?

The editor fields are the structured core. Add optional Markdown documentation when useful, but avoid contradictory duplicates in multiple places.

## Visibility and review

An app can be saved as a draft and continued later. For non-draft statuses, JustApps requires at least one category and a description. Depending on platform rules, submissions may first be reviewed by an administrator.

When handing over an app, update owner, editors, repository link, deployment variants, and contact together so the catalog entry stays aligned with actual responsibility.
