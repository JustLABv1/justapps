---
title: Collaboration and lifecycle
description: Maintain ownership, editors, groups, updates, and long-term app quality.
---

An app entry is a maintained product artifact. Agree on an owner and maintenance path before marking it established.

## Owner and editors

The owner is responsible for content and contact. Editors can update selected apps without taking ownership. Add editors when several people regularly contribute and remove them when teams change.

During a handover:

1. update the owner;
2. review editors and groups;
3. check repository access and provider links;
4. update the contact and support path;
5. confirm deployment, backup, and migration responsibilities.

## Groups and related apps

Groups support business navigation. Related apps show alternatives, complementary tools, or dependencies. Link only relationships that help readers and explain them in the description when needed.

## Status as lifecycle

Update the status as usage changes. An app should not remain **MVP** or **In Erprobung** forever after becoming established; conversely, a maintenance end or critical dependency should be visible as a regression.

Status changes should be reflected in documentation:

- **POC/MVP:** state goals and known gaps;
- **Sandbox:** clearly label test data and non-production use;
- **In Erprobung:** name the pilot scope and feedback path;
- **Etabliert:** document update, backup, and support models.

## Updates and changelog

For each relevant version, update at least description, deployment values, prerequisites, and links. When a repository is connected, review synchronized changes and approve them deliberately. Larger changes can be made in the editor or through the version/changelog feature; review AI changelog suggestions like any other proposal.

## Health and regular review

Review the entry after a release and on a fixed schedule:

- live demo and external links;
- repository sync and current paths;
- documentation age;
- owner and contact;
- visible warnings;
- startup of the documented Docker, Compose, or Helm variant.

Administrators see summarized signals under [App health](/verwaltung/katalog/gesundheit). The global link check can be disabled for protected demos, but the owner remains responsible for accuracy.

## Locking and archiving

An administrator can lock an app when it must not be changed temporarily. Do not delete an app merely because development has stopped: update its status and notices and document whether it is still operated or only archived. Export or otherwise archive content before deletion.
